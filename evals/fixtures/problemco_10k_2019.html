Item 1. Business

ProblemCo Holdings Inc. operates a global vehicle rental business through a network of company-operated and franchised locations in North America, Europe and other international markets. The Company rents cars, crossovers and light trucks under several brand names and also sells used vehicles through its retail sales channels.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

We have a substantial amount of indebtedness that could adversely affect our financial condition.

As of December 31, 2019 we had approximately $17 billion of total indebtedness, a substantial portion of which is secured by our vehicle fleet. Our substantial indebtedness could require us to dedicate a significant portion of our cash flow to debt service, limit our flexibility in planning for changes in our business and place us at a competitive disadvantage.

Competition in the vehicle rental industry could harm our results of operations.

The vehicle rental industry is highly competitive, with price being one of the primary competitive factors. Any change by our major competitors in their fleet sizes or pricing policies could have a material adverse effect on our revenues, results of operations and our ability to maintain or increase our market share.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2019 were $9,779 million, an increase of 3% compared to 2018, driven by higher volume in our Americas rental segment. The Company recorded a net loss of $58 million for the year, compared to a net loss of $225 million in 2018.
